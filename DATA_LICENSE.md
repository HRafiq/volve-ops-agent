## The dataset

The Equinor Volve open dataset, distributed by Equinor as the "Volve Data Village", is released
under the Equinor Open Data Licence. The terms document ships inside the share itself, and the
licence is published alongside the dataset at
<https://www.equinor.com/energy/volve-data-sharing>.

What follows is a summary of the terms as they stood when the data was retrieved on 2026-10-04.
The licence document itself states that its own summary is not a substitute for the licence, so
treat this the same way and read the terms before relying on them.

The licence permits downloading and using the material for commercial and non-commercial
purposes, producing adapted material from it, and sharing either. It requires that:

- the material is not sold;
- Equinor and the Volve licence partners are credited, with a link to the terms and a copyright
  notice where applicable;
- adapted material is not shared under a licence that would stop recipients complying with
  these terms;
- the material is not used in a way that appears misleading, nor presented in a distorted or
  incorrect form.

Equinor also states that the data is provided as is, may contain errors and omissions, and
carries no warranty as to content or relevance.

## What this repository does about it

No dataset file, raw or processed, is committed here. `docs/data_manifest.md` records what was
read, with checksums, so a reader can obtain the same inputs from Equinor directly rather than
from this repository.

No part of the dataset is sold or repackaged for distribution. Attribution is carried in
[NOTICE-VOLVE.md](NOTICE-VOLVE.md).

Figures derived from the data appear in this repository as analysis results. Where a document
is quoted as evidence, the quotation is bounded and attributed to its source document, and the
underlying file is not redistributed.
